### Session:12:15 (bluefin — why did the machine turn off? + dolt cleanup)

- **Verdict: it did not shut down — it lost power / was cut off.** Last journal line of the previous boot (`b6798b32…`) is `Thu 2026-11-26 12:00:56` mid-stream in `hourly-sync.sh` (`[gemini] listing failed`); there is **no systemd shutdown sequence** (no `Stopped target`, no `systemd-shutdown`), no kernel `reboot:` line, and **no `shutdown` record in wtmp** for the event. Next boot starts `12:02:02` — a **66 s gap**, i.e. it did not auto-reboot (that would be 5–20 s); a human pressed power again after it went dark.
- **Ruled out with evidence**: panic/lockup (no `BUG:`/`Oops`/`hard LOCKUP`/RCU stall in the whole 7-day boot; `/sys/fs/pstore` empty; kdump not loaded — `kexec_crash_loaded=0`); thermal (no thermal/throttle messages, TZ ~48 °C); OOM/load (sar: 88 % idle, 9 % user in the final interval); battery depletion (**89 % at 11:50, 80 % right after boot** — battery health 81.5 %, 34 cycles); failed suspend (last suspend was Nov 21 20:38, nothing at 12:00).
- **Positive evidence for an abrupt EC/firmware-level cut**: `upowerd` never flushed battery history (charge file stops `11:51:37` while the journal keeps writing for 9 more minutes) — an unclean death loses the in-memory history; and the `66 s` gap means a cold power-on, not an automatic reset.
- **Recurring, not a one-off**: boot `-3` also dies mid-stream (`Nov 18 21:32:19`, next boot 71 s later) and boot `-4` likewise; `last` records 105 boots as "crash". Boots `-2`/`-3` were on AC, this one was **on battery** (AC off since Nov 25 22:49). Hardware: `Framework Laptop 13 (AMD Ryzen 7040)`, BIOS `03.16`, EC `azalea_v3.4.113388`, Bluefin 44 immutable.
- **Not yet provable without root** (no passwordless sudo): the EC console survives AP power-off and usually names the cause. Next: `sudo mount -t debugfs none /sys/kernel/debug && sudo cat /sys/kernel/debug/cros_ec/console`, or `sudo ectool --interface=lpc console` / `ectool chipinfo` (reset flags). Also `fwupdmgr get-updates` — Framework shipped EC/BIOS shutdown fixes after 3.16. To capture the next event instead of nothing: `kernel.hardlockup_panic=1` / `softlockup_panic=1` and enable kdump.
- **Side finding — hibernation is silently broken**: Secure Boot lockdown denies it, but logind keeps asking — `Lockdown: systemd-logind: hibernation is restricted` fires all day (3× within 7 s at 11:22 on resume). Lid/idle policy points at hibernate and fails invisibly.
- **Side finding — `beads-dolt.service` had restarted 184,490 times** (every 3.2 s for the whole boot, ~6 journal lines per attempt): user unit pointed `WorkingDirectory` at the nonexistent `~/.local/share/beads/dolt` and `--config` at a nonexistent `config.yaml` → `status=200/CHDIR`. Root cause: it predates beads 1.3.0, which runs **Dolt embedded** (`.beads/embeddeddolt`, in-process, no `sql-server`); no repo on this machine configures server/shared-server mode. **Fix:** `systemctl --user disable --now`, unit file moved to `~/.local/state/removed-unit-files/beads-dolt.service.2026-11-26` (was not chezmoi-managed, nothing else referenced it), `daemon-reload`, empty `~/.local/share/beads/` removed. Verified `bd where` → `…/.beads/embeddeddolt` and `bd ready` returns issues, i.e. beads never needed the server.
- **Next:** read the EC console for the power-off reason (needs sudo), check BIOS/EC updates via fwupd, arm lockup panic + kdump, and decide the lid/idle policy (s2idle vs the currently-denied hibernate).
