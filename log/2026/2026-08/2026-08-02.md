  - **AudioContext leak fix**: try/finally ensures close() on decode error
  - **Stereo-to-mono averaging**: all channels averaged (not just channel 0)

  - **Download filename**: extension properly stripped, colons replaced
  - Updated `spec/functionality.md` and session notes to reflect all changes
- **Decisions:**
  - Whisper-tiny is the recommended model for phones + long audio

  - Timestamped chunks from pipeline are accumulated with offset per-segment
  - No service worker yet (model caching is handled by transformers.js internally in IndexedDB)
